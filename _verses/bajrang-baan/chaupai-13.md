story:
  en: This verse is from the Bajrang Baan, a powerful prayer dedicated to Lord Hanuman.

    who is known for his strength and valor. The verse emphasizes the faith of the
    devotee in the divine power of Lord Hanuman and his ability to protect them from
    evil forces.
  hi: यह छंद बजरंग बाण से है, जो भगवान हनुमान के लिए समर्पित एक शक्तिशाली प्रार्थना
