story:
  en: The verse is from the Bajrang Baan, a powerful hymn dedicated to Lord Hanuman.
    It is believed to have been composed by Tulsidas, a 16th-century poet-saint. The
    hymn is often recited for protection, strength, and victory over evil. The verse
    specifically calls upon Lord Hanuman to embody truth and to destroy evil, reflecting
    the belief in his divine power and righteousness.
  hi: यह श्लोक बजरंग बाण से है, जो भगवान हनुमान को समर्पित एक शक्तिशाली स्तोत्र है।
