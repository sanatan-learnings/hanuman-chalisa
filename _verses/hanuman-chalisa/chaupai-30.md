  en: 'This verse captures Hanuman''s dual role - nurturing protector of the good,
    fierce destroyer of evil. "Ram dulare" (Rama''s beloved) explains why he can perform

    teaches that protection of the righteous necessarily includes opposition to the
    wicked.

    से नहीं। यह सिखाता है कि धर्मियों की रक्षा में आवश्यक रूप से दुष्टों का विरोध
