literal_translation:
  en: Hanuman went towards the sun to read it, assuming it to be a fruit in his mind, he made a mess. His feet went behind in the sky, his mind engrossed, no order, no confusion, the monkey child roamed. Seeing the wonder, the protectors of the world, Hari, Har, and Bidhi, had amazement in their eyes, burden in their minds. With strength, heroic spirit, patience, and courage, Tulsi says, Hanuman holds the essence of all.
  hi: हनुमान सूर्य की ओर गए, मन में उसे फल समझकर, उन्होंने अव्यवस्था पैदा कर दी। उनके पैर आकाश में पीछे हो गए, मन मगन, कोई क्रम नहीं, कोई भ्रम नहीं, बंदर बच्चे ने घूमना शुरू कर दिया। आश्चर्य देख, लोकपाल, हरि, हर, और बिधि की आँखों में आश्चर्य था, मन में बोझ। बल, वीर रस, धीरज, और साहस के साथ, तुलसी कहते हैं, हनुमान सभी का सार धारण करता है।
interpretive_meaning:
  en: The verse symbolizes the innocent and playful nature of Hanuman as a child, his immense strength, and his ability to cause wonder and amazement among the gods. It also signifies that with courage, strength, patience, and a heroic spirit, one can hold the essence of all.

meaning: The verse describes the playful nature of Hanuman as a child. He goes towards the sun, thinking it to be a fruit, causing a mess. His mind is engrossed and he roams in the sky without any order or confusion.
