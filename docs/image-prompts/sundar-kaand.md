### Verse 8: Enter Lanka with Ram in Your Heart

**Scene Description**:
Hanuman standing at the threshold of Lanka's golden gates, poised to enter the demon capital with extraordinary determination and devotion. His powerful orange-red form glows with divine energy, but his expression shows humility and focus. The most striking element: a luminous, radiant image of Lord Rama emanates from Hanuman's heart chakra - golden-white divine light showing Rama in full glory, bow in hand, creating a protective aura around Hanuman. This visualization represents "hridayan rakhi Kosalapura raja" (keeping the King of Ayodhya in the heart). The massive ornate gates of Lanka tower before him - intricate golden architecture with demon guards visible in shadow. The city beyond shows palatial structures and ethereal demonic energy, creating contrast with Hanuman's divine light. Hanuman's posture embodies perfect karma yoga - ready for action ("prabisi nagara kije saba kaja") yet spiritually grounded. His hands are in anjali mudra (prayer gesture) toward his heart where Ram's image glows. Sacred Om symbols and "Ram" naam mantra letters flow from his heart outward, creating a protective shield. The scene shows the perfect balance: warrior readiness meeting spiritual devotion. Background reveals Lanka's grandeur and danger - golden spires, demon guards, foreboding atmosphere - yet Hanuman remains fearless with Ram in his heart. Dramatic lighting with divine rays emanating from Hanuman's heart contrasting with the darker, imposing architecture of Lanka. The composition emphasizes that true power comes from divine presence within, not external might. Mystical atmosphere showing the principle of karma yoga - complete action with complete devotion. Emphasis on entering challenging situations with both courage and spiritual grounding, demonstrating that when we keep the Divine in our hearts, we can face any adversity with fearlessness and wisdom.
